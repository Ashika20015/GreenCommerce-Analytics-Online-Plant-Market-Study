# 🌿 GreenCommerce Analytics – Online Plant Market Study

## 📌 Project Overview

GreenCommerce Analytics is an Excel-based data analytics project that explores the online plant market through product pricing, discounts, and category-wise analysis.

The project analyzes 397 online plant product listings across 12 different plant categories. Using Microsoft Excel, raw product data was collected, cleaned, organized, and transformed into meaningful business insights through Pivot Tables, charts, and an interactive dashboard.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze pricing patterns across different plant categories.
- Compare original prices with discounted selling prices.
- Identify average discounts offered on online plant products.
- Understand product distribution across different plant categories.
- Create an interactive Excel dashboard for business analysis.
- Extract meaningful insights to understand online plant market trends.

## 📊 Dataset Overview

- **Total Records:** 397
- **Plant Categories:** 12
- **Data Source:** Flipkart

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

The raw dataset was prepared for analysis through the following steps:

- Organized the collected plant product records.
- Cleaned product names and category labels.
- Removed currency text from price fields.
- Converted original and special prices into numeric formats.
- Standardized plant categories into 12 groups.
- Calculated discount percentages using Excel formulas.
- Prepared the cleaned dataset for Pivot Table analysis.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- **Product Distribution:** Fruit and Flower categories have the highest number of product listings.
- **Premium Pricing:** Bamboo has the highest average original price, approximately ₹755.
- **Customer Savings:** The average discount across the analyzed listings is 64%.
- **Market Opportunities:** Vegetable and Tree categories show potential opportunities for further product expansion.
- **Pricing Analysis:** The average special price (₹166.02) is considerably lower than the average original price (₹514.20).

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. **Data Collection:** Collected 397 online plant product listings using the Web Scraper Chrome Extension.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Cleaned and standardized the records using Microsoft Excel.
3. **Data Analysis:** Created Pivot Tables and calculated category-wise summaries.
4. **Dashboard Development:** Designed an interactive dashboard using Pivot Charts, KPI cards, and slicers.
5. **Business Insights:** Analyzed pricing, discounts, and product distribution to identify market trends.
